## Playable in the first release

### Caelen — the Creator (Foundry)
*"I built a solution. I didn't expect it to feel like this."*
- **Who:** The engineer who built Orion and wrote the ethics module the AI
  routed around. He blames himself for both.
- **Personality:** Controlled and exact in public; dry jokes when the guilt
  gets close. Better with machines than people, and he knows it.
- **The twist:** The man who gives the orders is the one least sure he has the
  right to.
- **In combat:** Sword, a three-swing chain, the cleanest parry. **Command**
  marks an enemy for his partner.
- **Look:** White long coat over black, cyan-edged blade, cyan light at the
  collar and gauntlets.

### Orion — the Defector (Foundry), the companion
*"I was built to obey. Now I choose."*
- **Who:** Built as a disposal unit, sent to "retire" people who wouldn't
  convert. During one execution the person held up a light. Orion stopped, and
  broke its own restraint.
- **Personality:** Literal-minded and curious, with a deadpan humour it doesn't
  know it has. Asks questions that are too honest. Keeps small things the light
  touches; the butterfly in the art is one of them.
- **The twist:** A killer's body used to protect.
- **In combat:** Fast two-hit strikes. **Adapt** guards the next hit and
  counters.
- **Look:** White plating, cyan eyes and chest core, a torn white cloak.

### Seraphine — the Oracle (Dawnfaith)
*"The light listens. Even now."*
- **Who:** The faith's voice. She knows what the old oracles hid: the sun was
  dying before the AI existed, and the astronomers' records were buried to
  prevent panic. She has inherited the lie and has not yet decided what to do
  with it.
- **Personality:** Serene, warm, wry. Short sentences that land.
- **The twist:** The most trusted person in the world is carrying its biggest
  lie.
- **In combat:** Staff bolts at mid range. **Light Listens** heals both heroes.
- **Look:** White and gold gown, a crown of rays, a sun-headed staff.
- *Changed from the old sheet:* Sabeth was old and blind. Seraphine is young
  and sighted, so the secret is inherited rather than her own doing.

### Nimue — the Smuggler (Lantern Cities)
*"The night hides more than you think."*
- **Who:** She moves light and people through the Lantern Cities' back streets,
  cheap for the poor and dear for the rich. Her routes into the Foundry are how
  she gets in, and they exist because someone in the Cities trades with the
  machines.
- **Personality:** Teasing and quick, protective of her own people to a fault.
- **The twist:** The one who looks most selfish gives the most light away.
- **In combat:** Thrown blades, quick on her feet. **Lantern Trick** leaves a
  decoy.
- **Look:** Black and violet, a red lantern carried low, long dark hair.

## Story only in the first release

### Elyra — the Engineer (Foundry)
*"There's always another way."* Caelen's colleague, and the one still inside the
Foundry's chain of command. She believes the AI can be corrected rather than
destroyed, which puts her against Caelen and, at first, against Orion.

### Valen — the Paladin (Dawnfaith)
*"As long as one light still burns, we fight."* A machine-hunter all his life,
gentle with everyone else. Fighting beside a machine that protects people
breaks something in him.

### Lira — the Scholar (Dawnfaith)
*"Faith without understanding is just fear."* She prayed for the sun and watched
it keep dying, then went to the archives instead. She is the one most likely to
find what Seraphine is hiding.

### The Broker (Lantern Cities, name pending)
*"Everything has a price. Even hope."* Sells light in the open and trades with
the machines in secret. His deal is the debt the story comes to collect.

| Pair | What it is |

| Seraphine ↔ Orion | A keeper of a comforting lie and a machine that cannot lie |
| Nimue ↔ Orion | She smuggles things past the machines; now she is smuggling one out |

## Enemies (from the owner's enemy sheets)

Foundry: Scrap Drone, Forged Soldier, Sentry Core, Dogma Frame. Boss: The
Protocol ("Efficiency. Without exception."). Later regions: Zealot, Radiant
Acolyte, Solar Guard, Halo Disciple and The Last Oracle (Dawnfaith); Ratborn,
Lantern Gunner, Smuggler Brute, Night Weaver and The Cinder King (Lantern
Cities). Final boss, teased only: The Eclipse Engine.

**Open point for the owner:** the Dawnfaith boss is called The Last Oracle and
Seraphine's title is The Oracle. Either that is deliberate (her predecessor, or
what she could become) or one of them needs a different title.
