# Open questions · v0.1

These are the decisions that matter most, roughly in order. They are for the creator to make; nobody fills them
in alone.

| # | Question | Why it matters | Options to start from |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **What do people see when the world fails?** | Anchors environments, collectibles, missions and the opening cinematic | **Partly decided:** the weakening sun is the main sign (LC-005). The other two symptoms below are still proposals |
| 2 | **How fast is it happening?** Years, generations, uneven waves? | Shapes how societies work and what ordinary people remember | Uneven waves let each region live a different stage |
| 3 | **What can magic do, and what can it never do?** | Keeps both forces meaningful | Draft limits in `Systems/MagicVsTechnology.md` |
| 4 | **What does magic cost?** | Costs should come out of character dilemmas, not a mana bar | Memory, vows, places, relationships, time (`Systems/Magic.md`) |
| 5 | **What was the AI originally asked to solve?** | Its mandate matters more than its power | Resource crisis, extinction risk, political order, all three; the AI's *belief* is known (WC-010), its original mandate is not |
| 6 | **How is the Dawnfaith organized?** Does Seraphine's gift bring rank or suspicion? | Shapes every Dawnfaith story | One church; competing orders; local sanctuaries |
| 7 | **Who governs the Lantern Cities?** | Shapes every Lantern story | Merchant council; guilds; criminal houses; no one |
| 8 | **How did the eight heroes meet, if they have?** | Don't assume they're allies | Separate stories that meet only in a crossover |
| 9 | **Who is the Last Oracle to Seraphine?** | Big plot lever | A different person; her predecessor; a rival; a title; what she could become |
| 10 | **What truth does the first game settle?** | Which local mystery ends with The Protocol, and which global questions stay open | What the line was making and why Caelen woke |

## Proposal for question 1 (PROPOSED)

Three symptoms seen in all three regions but read differently. Each can be shown on screen, put in a collectible,
or carry a mission.

| Symptom | Foundry reading | Dawnfaith reading | Lantern Cities reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The sun weakens:** shorter, colder days, a paler disc | A measurable energy decline to manage | Judgement, or the sacred bond fraying | Light itself becomes a commodity |
| **Things stop working, unevenly:** machines fail and some rituals fail too, not always together | Cascading system failure | Grace withdrawn from the unworthy | Somebody is taking the parts and the blessings |
| **Places go dark and stay dark:** districts where no light holds | Unmodelled dead zones to contain | Wounds in the world | Abandoned by the powerful on purpose |

The first row is now LOCKED (DEC-006). Rows two and three wait for the creator.
