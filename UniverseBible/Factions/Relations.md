# Between the factions (PROPOSED)

| Relationship | Public tension | Possible cooperation |
|---|---|---|
| Foundry ↔ Dawnfaith | Unverifiable miracles against coercive measurement | Emergency medicine, joint disaster response |
| Foundry ↔ Lantern Cities | Licensing, rationing, contraband technology | Repair networks, independent research, logistics |
| Dawnfaith ↔ Lantern Cities | Moral regulation against pragmatism | Sanctuary services, medicine, food distribution |

**Rule:** every alliance and betrayal is driven by a specific person or institution, never a fixed national
character.

**Working thread (from the 2026-10-22 spec and cast sheet):** someone in the Lantern Cities trades with the
Foundry's machines in secret. Nimue's routes into the Foundry exist because of it. The Broker's deal is the debt
the story comes to collect.
