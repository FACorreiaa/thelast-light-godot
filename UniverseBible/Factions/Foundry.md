# The Foundry

**Themes (ART):** science · control · redemption
**Colours (ART):** steel, navy, cyan; hostile machines glow red.
**Heroes (ART):** Caelen, Elyra, Orion.
**Enemy design (ART):** precise, modular, automated, relentlessly utilitarian.
**Boss (ART):** The Protocol.

## Central question

*If we could calculate the perfect future, would we have the right to impose it?*

Its stories are about artificial intelligence, responsibility, free will and unintended consequences.

## Explanation of the decline (PROPOSED)

The Cascade Hypothesis: instability spreads between systems, so unregulated anomalies need disciplined
containment. See `../COLLAPSE_MYSTERY.md`.

## Inside the Foundry (PROPOSED)

- **Politics:** engineers who want to restore the world, against administrators who treat lives as variables to
  optimise. Some machines may refuse orders, as Orion did.
- **Daily life:** workers depend on power quotas, health systems and automated logistics. Losing access to a
  machine can matter as much as losing a home.
- **Who governs:** UNKNOWN. How much the AI rules, and how much people still do, is open.

## Hazard for writers

Don't make every Foundry citizen a villain, or every scientist a machine worshipper.
