# The Lantern Cities

**Themes (ART):** trade · survival · opportunity
**Colours (ART):** ember, amber, coal black, copper; fabric awnings and hanging lanterns.
**Heroes (ART):** Jax, Nimue.
**Enemy design (ART):** scavenged weapons, improvised tools, heavy brutes, deception and traps.
**Boss (ART):** The Cinder King.

## Central question

*What is freedom worth when survival has a price?*

Its stories are about commerce, independence, organized crime, class and moral compromise.

## Explanation of the decline (PROPOSED)

The Stolen Light: institutions decide who gets food, power, trade and protection while claiming there isn't
enough. See `../COLLAPSE_MYSTERY.md`. The 2026-10-22 spec has them profiting from scarcity, with some trading
quietly with the machines.

## Inside the Cities (PROPOSED)

- **Politics:** free traders, mutual-aid networks, criminal syndicates and ordinary residents, each with their own
  idea of freedom.
- **Daily life:** rooftops, markets, smuggling routes, informal neighbourhoods, and hard choices between your own
  safety and everyone's.
- **Who governs:** UNKNOWN. Merchants, councils, guilds, criminal houses, or rival sovereignties? (Open
  Question 7)

## Hazard for writers

Don't reduce the Lantern Cities to a lawless backdrop. People have culture, families, festivals, crafts,
neighbourhoods, and reasons to love their home.
