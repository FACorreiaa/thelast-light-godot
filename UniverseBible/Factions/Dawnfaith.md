# The Dawnfaith

**Themes (ART):** faith · sacrifice · renewal
**Colours (ART):** ivory, luminous gold, warm stone; celestial circles and radiant architecture.
**Heroes (ART):** Seraphine, Valen, Lira.
**Enemy design (ART):** robes, staves, shields, sun imagery, supernatural radiance.
**Boss (ART):** The Last Oracle.

## Central question

*What are we willing to sacrifice for salvation?*

Its stories are about miracles, religious authority, doubt, devotion, and the line between faith and fanaticism.

## Explanation of the decline (PROPOSED)

The Severance: humanity's choices broke a sacred relationship with the world. See `../COLLAPSE_MYSTERY.md`.
The 2026-10-22 spec has them believe **the dying sun is a judgement and blame the Foundry's hubris** (the weakening sun itself is LOCKED, LC-005; their reading of it is PROPOSED).

## Inside the Dawnfaith (PROPOSED)

- **Politics:** healers and sincere believers, against zealots who turn doubt into treason. Scholars can believe
  magic is real and still challenge the clergy.
- **Daily life:** sanctuaries serve as hospitals and refuges. Rituals hold communities together even when the
  theology is disputed.
- **Organization:** UNKNOWN. One church, competing orders, or local sanctuaries? (Open Question 6)

## Critical question

*If miracles are real, does that make every command spoken in their name just?*

## Hazard for writers

Don't let real magic validate the Dawnfaith's leadership.
