# How magic and technology meet

**LOCKED (LC-001):** They are different forces. Everything else on this page is **PROPOSED** until the creator
approves it.

## The six rules (PROPOSED)

| Question | Proposed rule |
|---|---|
| Can machines cast magic? | Not through technology alone. Whether a *conscious* machine could be touched by the supernatural is UNKNOWN. |
| Can science detect magic? | It can measure some physical consequences, not explain the source. |
| Can magic damage machines? | Yes, but magic is not automatically the stronger power. |
| Can technology resist magic? | It can resist some observable effects (physical defences, countermeasures), not negate magic itself. |
| Does magic cost something? | Yes; every supernatural act carries a meaningful price or limit. What it is remains UNKNOWN. |
| Can either one solve everything? | No. Each has weaknesses the other can exploit. |

Two more guardrails:

- **Hybrid devices** can wrap a magically made part in a technological casing. They are two cooperating systems,
  not proof the forces are one.
- **Faith and magic are separate claims.** A miracle can be real and its interpreters still wrong.

## Why this matters for stories

**Neither science nor magic is automatically right.** The Foundry may see magic as dangerously unpredictable. The
Dawnfaith may see an AI as an unforgivable attempt to replace the divine. Each holds knowledge the world needs.

## Gameplay translation (PROPOSED)

- **Foundry levels:** security systems, precision hazards, machines that adapt, hacking, readable attack tells.
- **Dawnfaith levels:** ritual spaces, protective radiance, changed traversal, conditional buffs, supernatural
  bosses.
- **Lantern Cities levels:** improvised mixtures of mundane traps, stolen machines, rare magic, mobility and
  trade-offs.
- **Mixed fights:** the player tells a machine's interruptible mechanism from a spell with a different
  vulnerability window. Avoid a simple blue-against-gold damage chart.

## The question that could carry a game

Could Orion, a conscious machine, ever touch magic? It stays **UNKNOWN** on purpose. It ties the two forces,
the Foundry and the Dawnfaith (through Lira), and the defector's search for what he is.
