# Magic

**LOCKED (LC-001):** Magic is genuinely supernatural. It is not misunderstood advanced technology and no lore twist
may reduce it to that.

This doesn't mean magic is infallible, holy, unlimited, or understood by the people who use it.

## Working definition (WORKING)

Magic makes genuinely supernatural effects. Its reality is accepted; its origin and full rules are disputed.
People may learn practices, rituals and disciplines, but a supernatural act cannot be reduced to a model a machine
could run.

## What magic is not

- **Not faith itself.** A miracle can be real while an institution's reading of it is wrong (see
  `MagicVsTechnology.md`, rule 6).
- **Not owned by the Dawnfaith.** Nimue's art shows magic-like effects in the Lantern Cities; whether they are magic
  is UNKNOWN.
- **Not a stronger kind of technology.** It doesn't win by default.

## Open (UNKNOWN)

- Where does magic come from: people, entities, places, vows, or something deeper?
- Does every spell have a price? Who pays, and when? (Open Question 4)
- Can someone use magic while rejecting the Dawnfaith's theology?
- Is it learned, inherited, or both?
- Does the decline weaken magic and the physical world independently, or are the failures linked?

## Candidate costs (PROPOSED, for the creator to choose)

The cost should come out of characters' dilemmas, not a mana bar. Candidates from the draft: fatigue, time,
memory, vows, rare places, relationships, sacrifice. One proposal from the direction notes: **powerful magic
consumes memories.**
