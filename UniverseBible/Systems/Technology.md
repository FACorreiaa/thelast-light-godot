# Technology, AI and engineering

## Working definition (WORKING)

Technology works through the measurable physical world: machines, matter, computation, tools, networks and energy
systems. It can be copied, automated, manufactured and scaled. It is limited by resources, maintenance,
fragility, environmental cost, imperfect information, and flawed human or AI goals.

## The Foundry's AI (WORKING)

- An AI tied to the Foundry is pursuing a solution to the dying world at terrible cost (WC-007).
- **Its diagnosis is not automatically right.** Its certainty is a character trait, not proof.
- The Protocol is its first direct voice in physical form (ART). The Eclipse Engine is described in the art as
  its final solution: a world without life.
- **WORKING (DEC-019):** in the 2D game the Engine's Heart is beaten and the Engine stands unfinished; the AI then
  speaks to the lead for the first time, not through the Protocol. Its words are proposals (DRAFT-013) and must not
  settle its mandate or history (open question 5).
- **WORKING (WC-010):** it believes organic life burns too much of the remaining light, and is replacing living
  things with machines that need none. That is its belief, not the truth.

## Machines with minds (WORKING)

Orion is a humanoid machine that chose to stop obeying (ART quote: "I was built to obey. Now I choose."). Machine
consciousness exists in this world. Whether all machines have it, and whether a conscious machine can be touched
by the supernatural, is **UNKNOWN**.
