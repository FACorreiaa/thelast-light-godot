# The Eclipse Engine (ART concept)

The AI's final solution, described in the art as **a world without life**. A multi-stage final conflict with
reality shifts and a catastrophic finale.

- **Rule:** its plan is an antagonist's choice, not a cosmic necessity.
- **Gameplay vs lore:** an attack called "Reality Pulse" doesn't prove the AI has mastered reality. Write the
  in-world explanation separately if it matters.
- **Decided (DEC-011):** game text names it without saying it is a running machine. The 2D game's chapter end:
  "The Protocol is silent. It was only a voice. The Eclipse Engine has not stopped."
- **WORKING (DEC-016, 2026-11-19):** in the 2D game's final chapter it is a work in progress: a vast half-built structure
  rising over a drowned city under the eclipse. It is not finished, so it can be stopped. The chapter climbs it and the
  boss guards its heart.
- **WORKING (DEC-019, 2026-11-25):** in this game the Heart is beaten; the Engine stands unfinished and silent over
  the water, and the sun is no brighter. The AI that built it then speaks to the lead for the first time, not
  through the Protocol (its lines: DRAFT-013, PROPOSED). Whether the decline can be reversed, whether the Engine can
  still be finished, and whether the AI survives stay UNKNOWN.
- **UNKNOWN:** the drowned city's name and history, what the Engine does when finished, and who designed it beyond the AI.

## The final chapter's machines (DEC-019, WORKING unless marked)

- **Stand-in:** a machine in the shape of someone who lived in the Still Quarter, keeping their routine. What
  became of the people stays open.
- **Scaffold Crawler:** a builder machine that climbs the Engine's frame. (Dropping loose parts is a gameplay idea, PROPOSED; not built.)
- **The Mason** (mid-boss): the machine building the span to the Heart; speaks no lines. How it fights is gameplay
  (see `../Games/TheLastLight2D.md`).
- **The Heart** (boss): has no face; fights through the eclipse's rhythm in three phases (gameplay). The voice that
  speaks after it is beaten is the AI's (DEC-019); how that voice reaches the lead is part of DRAFT-013.
