## Mid-boss: The Line Warden (WORKING, DEC-014)

**Not in the concept art; approved by the creator.** A tall soldier frame with a tower shield and a long blade that keeps the gate between the
Machine Hall and the Protocol. Orion knows it by name. Whether it is a Forged Soldier pattern, a one-off, or one of
many is UNKNOWN.

- **Game only:** its guard faces forward and stays up through its own swing; it turns slowly, so the way in is from
  behind or after a parry. Hits don't make it flinch. It spins if someone lingers behind it. In overdrive it turns
  faster and calls a Forged Soldier.

## Boss: The Protocol (ART)

The AI's first direct voice in physical form. Multi-phase transformations, adaptive behaviour, summons, energy
slashes, drones, laser rain and big arena attacks. Its line: "Efficiency. Without exception."

- **Shipped:** Orion says it "judged me once" (SH-004).
- **Game only:** three phases named Judgement, Ascension, Execution; armour that opens only after it strikes.
- **Rule:** The Protocol's certainty does not prove its diagnosis of the collapse.
