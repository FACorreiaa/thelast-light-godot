## In the games

## Decided (WORKING)

- She comes into the Foundry by its outer works to see the Sun Gauges, where it measures the light's loss; its numbers
  match her buried records (WC-012, DEC-012).
- Her order calls the Lantern Cities godless; on the docks she finds sanctuary bread resold at three times its cost,
  and the Cinder King's ledgers carry the Dawnfaith's seal (WC-016, WC-018, DEC-013).
- At the Eclipse Engine: "The sky is darker over it. Even the corona looks tired." To the AI's voice: "The Oracle
  wanted everyone still. So do you. Neither of you asked." (WC-028, DEC-020; says nothing of who the Oracle is to her)
- Her epilogue (ALTERNATE, DEC-021): "Home, with a Foundry machine beside me and the old records in my bag. Let
  them sing or not."

## Personality (PROPOSED)

Serene, warm, wry. Short sentences that land. *(cast sheet)*

## History (PROPOSED)

The faith's voice. She inherited what the old oracles hid: records buried to prevent panic, which show the light failing **earlier than official history admits**. They say when, not why. She hasn't decided what to do with it. *(WORKING, DEC-008; the rest of this section is PROPOSED)*

## Motivation (PROPOSED)

To act on a real calling without handing authority to its interpreters.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- **Strengths:** Trusted; a real gift. *(PROPOSED)*
- **Weaknesses:** The secret she keeps. *(PROPOSED)*

## Personal conflict (PROPOSED)

*Can she trust a real supernatural calling without giving unquestioned authority to those who interpret it?*

## Arc (PROPOSED)

Learns to tell compassion, revelation and institution apart. *Twist (cast sheet):* the most trusted person in the world carries its biggest lie.
