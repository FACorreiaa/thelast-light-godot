## In the games

**SHIPPED:** the lead of the 2D game's Chapter 1. Wakes on a line where the others never woke; Orion offers him the way out (SH-002). His special is **Command**: he marks an enemy and his partner focuses it (SH-005).

## Decided (WORKING)

  DEC-012). Why stays UNKNOWN, and his backstory stays PROPOSED below.
- In the Cinder King's ledgers he finds Foundry seals beside everyone else's (WC-018, DEC-013).

## Personality (PROPOSED)

Controlled and exact in public; dry jokes when the guilt gets close. Better with machines than people, and he knows it. *(cast sheet)*

## History (PROPOSED)

The engineer who built Orion and wrote the ethics module the AI routed around. He blames himself for both. *(cast sheet; see DEC-010)*

## Motivation (PROPOSED)

To undo a decision tied to the AI.

## Strengths and weaknesses

- **Strengths:** Precision; understands machines, including Orion. *(PROPOSED)*
- **Weaknesses:** Guilt that turns into control; distance from people. *(PROPOSED)*

## Personal conflict (PROPOSED)

*Can a creator take responsibility without claiming the right to control everyone else's future?*

## Arc (PROPOSED)

He learns that guilt alone is not accountability. *Twist (cast sheet):* the man who gives the orders is the one least sure he has the right to.

## Possible endings (ALTERNATE once written)

Atones through service; dismantles his own legacy; accepts hard stewardship without authoritarian control.

## Relationships (PROPOSED)

- Orion: maker and made, guilt and forgiveness. The spine of the Foundry story (cast sheet). The bible keeps the literal creator link optional (DEC-010).
- Elyra: destroy the AI or fix it; trust tested by secrecy.
