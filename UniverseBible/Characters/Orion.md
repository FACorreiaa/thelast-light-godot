## Personality (PROPOSED)

Literal-minded and curious, with a deadpan humour he doesn't know he has. Asks questions that are too honest. Keeps small things the light touches; the butterfly in the art is one. *(cast sheet)*

## History (PROPOSED)

Built as a disposal unit, sent to retire people who wouldn't convert. During one execution the person held up a light. He stopped, and broke his own restraint. *(cast sheet; see DEC-010)*

## Motivation (PROPOSED)
