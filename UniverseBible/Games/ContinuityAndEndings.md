# Continuity, endings and DLC

## Franchise model (LOCKED, LC-004)

Standalone games follow different characters and events in **one shared universe**. They connect through
consistent history, recognizable places, the magic and technology rules, and the faction conflicts. A player
doesn't need to finish one to enjoy the next.

Supported shapes: standalone games, parallel stories, prequels, personal character endings, non-canon player
choices, and crossovers where it makes sense.

## Three levels of truth

1. **Shared canon:** history, laws and relationships the creator has approved (`CANON.md`, LOCKED).
2. **Local game canon:** what is guaranteed inside one game: its opening, its required fights, its SHIPPED text.
3. **Player continuity:** optional allies, relationship outcomes, choices and endings in one playthrough
   (ALTERNATE).

## Endings

A character ending records what **this playthrough's hero** chose and became. It doesn't make every later game
accept it as history.

A later game can mention that a region is being rebuilt without saying whether Caelen died, became its
guardian, or left. If a story truly needs one prior outcome, that is a new creator decision, logged before
writing starts.

## DLC (PROPOSED)

- A DLC is a complete small story, not the missing end of the base game.
- New playable heroes bring distinct movement and combat, not just different damage colours.
- DLC lore goes through the same labels and decision log.
- Keep source models, rigs and animations in a shared asset library, even if a game ships only pre-rendered
  sprites. The code side is in `docs/architecture.md`.

## Checklist before a game ships

- [ ] Does it contradict anything LOCKED?
- [ ] Does it reveal the unknown cause of the collapse by accident?
- [ ] Does it force an earlier optional ending to be canon?
- [ ] Does it keep magic and technology distinct?
- [ ] Do its own heroes get a complete arc?
- [ ] Does every faction in it contain credible disagreement?
- [ ] Is every new world fact either in `CANON.md` or marked local to this game?
